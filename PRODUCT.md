# PRODUCT.md — BIG JADDY ENT Event Center

register: brand

## Product purpose

A one-page marketing site for **BIG JADDY ENT Event Center**, the event-center
arm of Big Jaddy Enterprise. The site's job is to make a prospective host feel
the venue's promise the moment they land, and to get them to **book a tour** or
**get in touch**. It is a marketing surface, not an app: the design IS the
product.

The event center is one of several Big Jaddy lines of business; for now the site
concentrates only on event-center services. Others may be added later.

## Users

People planning an event in Accra, Ghana: couples and families planning weddings
and engagements, churches running programs, companies booking conferences and
seminars, and hosts of parties and milestone celebrations. They are deciding
where to spend a meaningful budget on a once-in-a-while occasion, so trust,
warmth, and a sense of occasion matter more than feature lists.

## Brand & tone

Tagline: **"Where every event feels Grand."**

Three voice words: **stately, warm, ceremonial.** Think a gold-embossed
invitation on heavy navy card, or a gala program. Confident and gracious, never
corporate or clinical. Copy is concise and welcoming; it sells the feeling of a
well-run, beautiful day, and the relief of a stress-free host.

## What we offer (services)

- A versatile, serene event space
- Event décor and setup
- Rental packages: chairs, tables, canopies
- On-demand catering through trusted partners
- Professional photography and videography
- Optional sound and lighting

## Location

Achimota, Kingsby, adjacent Hof Vilac International School, Accra, Ghana.

## Anti-references

- Wedding-website cliché: blush-pink scripts, swirly fonts, doily textures.
- Generic SaaS landing template: centered icon-title-text card grids.
- The industrial/manufacturing look of the reference screenshot the client
  shared. We borrowed its modern structure, not its mood.

## Strategic principles

- Honor the logo: deep navy + gold carry the brand. Commit to color; do not
  hedge into beige.
- Imagery leads. An event center without real, warm event photography reads as
  incomplete. Prefer authentic African / Ghanaian celebration imagery.
- One strong page, easy to maintain. Simple is a feature here.
